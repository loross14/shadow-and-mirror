I work on blockchain and cryptography. Right now I'm building Planisphere — attestation infrastructure that turns high-stakes automated work into tamper-evident receipts anyone can independently verify, without ever holding your data.

Alongside it I write Shadow & Mirror, a carrier-qualified introduction to complexity, readback, and proof — the discipline of naming what carries a claim before you let it cross from one domain into another. The essays below are the reader-facing way in; the formal records live on Zenodo. I also maintain grapheine, a toolkit for measuring the structure of knowledge graphs.

A decade in and around startups and cryptography: CS at the University of Michigan, where I ran the campus blockchain club; crypto lead at Benzinga; developer content at Alchemy. A tenured startup vet who went from explaining the space to building the cryptography underneath it.
