Field guides to the corpus

Each entry mirrors one Zenodo record. Zenodo is the academic stamp; this surface is the reader's way in — the question, the method or measurement, the limits, and the shape of the idea in plain English.

  • Decipherment · Rapa Nui · zenodo.19362491

    Rongorongo and the Shape of an Undeciphered Script

    An LLM-driven structural reading of Easter Island's script — an 89-entry dictionary covering 96.9% of tokens, the Mamari tablet as eclipse calendar. Structure classified, not meanings assigned.

  • Decipherment · Indus Valley · zenodo.19362547

    Reading the Indus Valley Script

    A structural reading of the Indus corpus — full-sign coverage, positional grammar, co-occurrence. It classifies the corpus as language-like versus label-like; it assigns no meanings.

  • Decipherment · Proto-Elamite · zenodo.19362527

    Reading Proto-Elamite

    A full-sign dictionary and administrative-complexity reading of the Proto-Elamite corpus — a structural classifier, not a semantic decipherment.

  • AI Systems · Adapter Fleets · zenodo.20372606

    Behaviour-Space Spectral Geometry

    Direct variance attribution and bounded attestation for fine-tuned adapter fleets, measured through behaviour rather than weights.

  • Foundations · The Theorem · zenodo.19448861

    The Equals Sign as a Rotation

    The proven floor of the whole project: why V²+D²=1 is a theorem, not a metaphor — forced by orthogonality and Parseval, a circle and not a line, one degree of freedom.

  • Graph Theory · Separator Growth · zenodo.20649768

    The Interval-Graph Collapse: Separator Growth in One-Dimensional Point Processes

    On a line, distance-threshold graphs are unit interval graphs. Treewidth collapses to maximal window occupancy, turning separator growth into an extreme counting statistic.

  • Number Theory · Riemann Zeros · zenodo.19449047

    Riemann, Repulsion, and the Collapse of Treewidth on the Line

    The Riemann zeros as a measured repulsive spectrum: treewidth is not a global gap here, but suppressed local separator growth. It neither assumes nor proves RH.
